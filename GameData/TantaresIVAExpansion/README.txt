TANTARES IVA EXPANSION
======================

Realistic internal views (IVA) for the Soyuz, Progress and some Mir/ISS modules
of the Tantares mod, for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x.

- Reviva-switchable IVAs for Soyuz (T, TM, TMA, M, MS)
- IVA for Soyuz LOK (Virgo)
- Station module IVAs loosely inspired by real interiors
- Working controls powered by MAS, FreeIVA compatible
- The original Tantares IVAs stay available in the IVA switch (default)

REQUIREMENTS
------------
Tantares, ASET Props, MOARdV Avionics Systems, FreeIVA,
Reviva, B9 Part Switch, Module Manager.

Optional (Mir/ROS modules): HabTech2, Station Parts Expansion Redux (IVAs only).

INSTALLATION
------------
Copy the TantaresIVAExpansion folder into your GameData folder.
